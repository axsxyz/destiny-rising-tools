This is the general tier list for all the Artifacts in the game. It should be noted that rankings are based on the Artifact by itself, and some Artifacts may become significantly better when used by specific characters or builds.

Artifact Ranking Criteria

Artifacts in the S-Tier are strong, regardless of game mode or character using it. It's recommended to farm for these first since you can put them on any character before you start farming for more optimized builds.
A-Tier Artifacts are very good, but are just outside the S-Tier. Also, Artifacts in this tier can be comparable to those in S when used with the right character or build.
Artifacts ranked in the B-Tier are okay, but can't compete with those in S or A. However, they can still be very useful in certain scenarios, builds, or party setups.
C-Tier Artifacts should be avoided unless you want to use them, or you're using them temporarily to complete a build. Generally, though, these will be used as upgrade fodder unless you want to keep them for future characters that may be able to use them.

This is an explanation for the criteria used for Artifact rankings. Depending on new and upcoming content, Artifact rankings can change or shift depending on the criteria listed above.
